# Serena L. Wang 

<img src="img/serena.jpg" alt="Photo" class="rightside_image">

I am a postdoc at [Harvard](https://crcs.seas.harvard.edu/people/serena-wang), hosted by [Ariel Procaccia](https://procaccia.info/). In 2026, I will be joining the [University of British Columbia](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/) as an Assistant Professor in Computer Science. 

I have also concurrently worked with [Google Research](https://research.google/people/SerenaLutongWang/) at 20% time for the last seven years, where I have had the privilege of working with [Ravi Kumar](https://sites.google.com/site/ravik53/), [Preston McAfee](https://mc4f.ee/), and [Maya Gupta](https://mayagupta.org/). I received my PhD in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://eecs.berkeley.edu/), advised by [Michael I. Jordan](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~jordan/). My PhD research was supported by the Apple Scholars in AI/ML PhD Fellowship and the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship. 

My research focuses on understanding and improving the long term societal impacts of AI by rethinking algorithms and their surrounding incentives and practices. Recently, I've been particularly interested in evaluation processes for AI systems and beyond, including how to mitigate gaming [[WBAJ'25]](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v238/wang24b/wang24b.pdf), how to discover new metrics [[WJLM'25]](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3696410.3714864) [[WBKLMV'25]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16655), and how to think about representation and fairness [[PSWZ'25]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09813). My research combines ideas from AI/ML, economics, statistics, and optimization.
